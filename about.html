About the publication

Small by design.

Carbone Notes is an independent publication for people who want the context behind technical change.

We write about technology, cybersecurity, science and digital culture without treating every new product or headline as a revolution.

The editorial rule is simple: state what is known, separate it from interpretation, and make the useful part understandable. That sometimes means a guide; sometimes it means a close reading of a security problem; sometimes it means admitting that an impressive laboratory result is still a long way from everyday infrastructure.

Carbone Notes is intentionally light on machinery. The site is static HTML and CSS, with a small Python publishing system behind the automated editions. The publication should feel more like a notebook on a good desk than a software product brochure.
